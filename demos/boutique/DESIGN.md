# Offscript

Brief: a small fashion capsule for everyday dressing. Taste-skill variance7, motion2, density4. Crisp monochrome canvas, slate-blue photography, squared control system, bold wordmark and large garment imagery. Two products are deliberately presented as a small capsule, not fabricated inventory.

References visually reviewed in Chrome before implementation:
- Maria Osadcha, [Fashion E-commerce: Editorial Luxury](https://dribbble.com/shots/27413325-Fashion-E-commerce-Website-Editorial-Luxury-UI-Design): curated garment focus and editorial image scale; independent typography and palette.
- Alex Kozyuk, [QUIET Fashion E-commerce](https://www.behance.net/gallery/254133157/QUIET-Fashion-E-commerce-Website): monochrome hierarchy, restrained navigation and strong fashion art direction. No copied layouts, models, product descriptions or assets.

Built-in imagegen generated all local assets:
- editorial.webp, source exec-3059c62e-b130-4723-9bc8-ab048d76fc40.png: original full-length adult woman wearing slate-blue overshirt, charcoal wide-leg trousers, white shoes against light architectural concrete, candid professional fashion photography, no logos or text.
- overshirt.webp, source exec-5abba65a-a9fa-42ff-a3b2-158bb49e7ba4.png: single slate-blue cotton overshirt with collar/buttons/pockets flat on cool gray seamless background, complete garment, catalogue photo, no text/people/logo.
- tote.webp, source exec-85b8e448-f7dc-4f02-a43a-470db2453cc9.png: sculptural black canvas tote upright on cool gray seamless background, whole bag, fine texture, studio shadow, no text/people/logo.
All images optimized to WebP. Styling is illustrative; the hero is an editorial scene, not a literal product SKU match.

Features: category/search filters, empty/reset, product dialogs, size validation, browser-local shortlist, quantity1–5, removal, subtotal, honest enquiry simulation and text download. Stored records are restricted to known product IDs/sizes and valid quantities. No PII, analytics, external requests, checkout or real stock. Buildless native web stack; reduced motion and dark tokens.

# Forma Rituals

Brief inference: a premium salon for visitors seeking an unhurried self-care appointment. Taste-skill variance7, motion2, density4. Original pale-rose/berry identity, roundedgeometry, light Manrope display typography, arch-shaped interior photography and an actual service planner. This differs deliberately from the dark restaurant and serif hotel.

## References inspected before implementation
- [Raddito Agency, Nail & Beauty Salon Website Design](https://dribbble.com/shots/26905072-Nail-Beauty-Salon-Website-Design), viewed visually in Chrome: service pricing clarity, calm spacing and photography grounded the service-led layout. No borrowed products, claims or imagery.
- [Palletio Design Studio and collaborators, Global Spa](https://www.behance.net/gallery/245099601/Global-Spa-Luxury-Wellness-Therapy-Website-Design), viewed visually in Chrome: integrated service/date/time booking inspired an actual interactive planner. We used an independent rose palette and no clinical outcomes, real therapist imagery or fabricated credentials.

## Working interaction
Four service choices update price and duration. Date picker and upcoming-day buttons, Sunday closure, next-day through30-day window. Slots disabled when a service would exceed18:00closing. Incompatible selected slots clear when services change; dates reset time. Native modal confirms exact selections as local simulation. FAQ disclosure, visible focus, automatic dark tokens and reduced motion. No medical advice, real bookings, payments, PII or external runtime requests.
